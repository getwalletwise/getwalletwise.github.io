Why I built this

It started with one question at home.

I'm Juan. In our house, I'm the one who runs the money — and my wife kept asking me the same thing: “which card should I use for this?”

So I built her a simple guide: one tap, the right card. She stopped guessing, and we kept earning — in my own wallet it's added up to roughly $1,000–$2,500 back a year (it depends on how you spend), without spending a single dollar more.

Then it clicked: anyone with more than one card has this exact problem, and fixing it costs nothing — just a smarter tap. That's the whole idea. Use what you already have, wisely. Wallet-wise.

Good questions

The things people ask first.

Is my card information safe?

Yes. I only use the last 3 digits and a nickname to draw your cards — never full numbers. Nothing you type into the app is saved or sent anywhere; it runs entirely on your phone.

Do I have to spend more to benefit?

No — that's the whole point. WalletWise is built around the spending you already do. You just route each purchase to the card that pays you back the most.

What happens when my cards change?

Tell me and I'll update your app. Card reward rules shift over time too, so I refresh everything on a quarterly schedule at no extra charge.

Does it work on Android?

Yes. It runs in any phone browser and installs to the home screen on both iPhone and Android — the steps differ slightly, and I'll walk you through it.

Is this financial advice?

No. It's a convenience tool for using the cards you already own. I don't sell cards and I don't earn commissions — the recommendation is simply the best match for your wallet.
